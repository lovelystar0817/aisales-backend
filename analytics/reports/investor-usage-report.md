## Appendix B: Methodology

**Data Sources:**
- People AI: Production database (Sept 7 - Oct 7, 2025)
- Sales AI: Production database (Sept 7 - Oct 7, 2025)
- Analysis conducted: October 7, 2025

**Data Processing:**
- Test users excluded (email pattern: +*@hupo.co)
- Session duration hierarchy: roleplay.duration → timestamp difference → 10 sec default
- Anomaly handling: Sessions >30 minutes capped at 10 minutes
- Time zones: All timestamps in UTC

**Metric Definitions:**
- **Active User:** ≥1 session in period
- **Session:** Single login instance with activity
- **Engagement Time:** Sum of all session durations
- **Login Days:** Count of unique calendar dates with activity

**Limitations:**
- Point-in-time analysis (30-day window)
- Usage metrics only (no outcome/revenue data)
- Self-reported session quality not captured
- Technical errors/incomplete sessions included
