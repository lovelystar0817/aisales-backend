## Data Quality Notes

### People AI
- **Duration Calculation:** Uses `roleplay.duration` field with normalization
- **Normalization:** Durations > 1800 seconds (30 min) are capped at 600 seconds (10 min)
- **Default Duration:** 10 seconds for sessions without valid duration data
- **Data Completeness:** Some sessions show 0.17 min durations, indicating possible quick check-ins or incomplete sessions

### Sales AI
- **Duration Calculation:** Same as People AI - uses `roleplay.duration` with fallback to timestamp difference
- **Test User Filter:** Automatically excludes emails matching `+*@hupo.co`
- **Company Focus:** Prudential (UOB Group users), Grab, Manulife
- **Data Integrity:**
  - Normalized durations handle corrupt data (e.g., Felicia @ Grab had 1.7 billion seconds normalized to 10 min)
  - Some sessions show 0.17 min (10 sec default) when both roleplay.duration and endedAt are missing
- **Note:** Prudential users are from UOB Group (emails ending with @uobgroup.com)
